Example 1 Math

Problem: A block of mass 680g fastened to spring k=65 N/m, pulled 11cm. Find (a) \(\omega\), f, T; (b) A; (c) v_max.

📝 Show Solution

(a) Angular frequency, frequency, and period

Formula: \( \omega = \sqrt{\frac{k}{m}} \)

Substitution: \( \omega = \sqrt{\frac{65}{0.680}} = 9.78 \text{ rad/s} \)

Formula: \( f = \frac{\omega}{2\pi} \)

Substitution: \( f = \frac{9.78}{2\pi} = 1.56 \text{ Hz} \)

Formula: \( T = \frac{1}{f} \)

Substitution: \( T = \frac{1}{1.56} = 0.64\text{ s} \)

(b) Amplitude

Formula: \( A = \text{initial displacement} \)

Substitution: \( A = 0.11\text{ m} \)

(c) Maximum speed

Formula: \( v_{max} = \omega A \)

Substitution: \( v_{max} = (9.78)(0.11) = 1.1 \text{ m/s} \)

Example 2 Math

Problem: Spring stretches 3.90cm with 10.0g. 25.0g oscillates. Find (a) T; (b) f, \(\omega\).

📝 Show Solution

First, find the spring constant \( k \):

Formula: \( k = \frac{F}{\Delta y} = \frac{m_1 g}{\Delta y} \)

Substitution: \( k = \frac{0.010 \times 9.8}{0.039} = 2.513 \text{ N/m} \)

(a) Period of oscillation

Formula: \( T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{m_2}{k}} \)

Substitution: \( T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{0.025}{2.513}} = 0.627\text{ s} \)

(b) Frequency and angular frequency

Formula: \( f = \frac{1}{T} \)

Substitution: \( f = \frac{1}{0.627} = 1.59\text{ Hz} \)

Formula: \( \omega = 2\pi f \)

Substitution: \( \omega = 2\pi(1.59) = 10.0 \text{ rad/s} \)

Example 3 Math

Problem: 0.42kg, spring stretches 2.1cm, a=9.0 m/s². Find k.

📝 Show Solution

Find the spring constant \( k \):

Formula: \( F = ma = kx \implies k = \frac{ma}{x} \)

Substitution: \( k = \frac{0.42 \times 9.0}{0.021} = 180 \text{ N/m} \)

Example 4 Math

Problem: m=0.30kg, k=19.6N/m, A=0.100m. Find (a) E; (b) K,U vs t; (c) v at x=0.050m; (d) K,U at x=A/2.

📝 Show Solution

(a) Total Energy

Formula: \( E = \frac{1}{2}kA^2 \)

Substitution: \( E = \frac{1}{2}(19.6)(0.100)^2 = 9.80 \times 10^{-2} \text{ J} \)

(b) Potential & Kinetic Energy as a function of time

Formula: \( \omega = \sqrt{\frac{k}{m}} \)

Substitution: \( \omega = \sqrt{\frac{19.6}{0.30}} = 8.08 \text{ rad/s} \)

\( U(t) = \frac{1}{2}kA^2 \cos^2(\omega t) = 9.80 \times 10^{-2} \cos^2(8.08t) \text{ J} \)

\( K(t) = \frac{1}{2}kA^2 \sin^2(\omega t) = 9.80 \times 10^{-2} \sin^2(8.08t) \text{ J} \)

(c) Velocity at x = 0.050m

Formula: \( v = \pm \omega \sqrt{A^2 - x^2} \)

Substitution: \( v = \pm (8.08)\sqrt{0.100^2 - 0.050^2} = \pm 0.70 \text{ m/s} \)

(d) Kinetic and Potential Energy at x = A/2 = 0.050m

Formula: \( U = \frac{1}{2}kx^2 \)

Substitution: \( U = \frac{1}{2}(19.6)(0.050)^2 = 2.45 \times 10^{-2} \text{ J} \)

Formula: \( K = E - U \)

Substitution: \( K = 9.80 \times 10^{-2} - 2.45 \times 10^{-2} = 7.35 \times 10^{-2} \text{ J} \)

Example 5 Math

Problem: 500g, A=20cm, T=0.80s. At what x is v=1.0 m/s?

📝 Show Solution

Find angular frequency \( \omega \):

Formula: \( \omega = \frac{2\pi}{T} \)

Substitution: \( \omega = \frac{2\pi}{0.80} = 7.85 \text{ rad/s} \)

Find position \( x \) where \( v = 1.0 \text{ m/s} \):

Formula: \( v = \pm \omega \sqrt{A^2 - x^2} \implies x = \pm \sqrt{A^2 - \left(\frac{v}{\omega}\right)^2} \)

Substitution: \( x = \pm \sqrt{0.20^2 - \left(\frac{1.0}{7.85}\right)^2} = \pm 15.4 \text{ cm} \)

Example 6 Math

Problem: Loudspeaker f=1.0kHz, A=0.20mm. (a) v_max; (b) where.

📝 Show Solution

Formula: \( v_{max} = A\omega = A(2\pi f) \)

Substitution: \( v_{max} = (0.20 \times 10^{-3})(2\pi \times 1.0 \times 10^3) = 1.3 \text{ m/s} \)

(b) Where it occurs

The maximum speed occurs as the object passes through the equilibrium position.

Result: \( \text{at } x = 0 \)

Example 10 Math

Problem: Glider pulled 20cm, 15 oscillations in 10s. (a) T; (b) v_max; (c) x,v at t=0.80s.

📝 Show Solution

(a) Period

Formula: \( T = \frac{\text{total time}}{\text{number of oscillations}} \)

Substitution: \( T = \frac{10}{15} = 0.667 \text{ s} \)

(b) Maximum velocity

First find \( \omega \): \( \omega = \frac{2\pi}{T} = \frac{2\pi}{0.667} = 9.42 \text{ rad/s} \)

Formula: \( v_{max} = \omega A \)

Substitution: \( v_{max} = (9.42)(0.20) = 1.88 \text{ m/s} \)

(c) Position and velocity at t = 0.80s

The equation of motion is \( x(t) = 20\text{cm} \cos(9.42t) \)

Substitution for x: \( x(0.80) = 20 \cos(9.42 \times 0.80) = 6.2 \text{ cm} \)

The velocity equation is \( v(t) = -v_{max} \sin(\omega t) \)

Substitution for v: \( v(0.80) = -1.88 \sin(9.42 \times 0.80) = -1.79 \text{ m/s} \)

Example 11 Math

Problem: Starts at x=A. When first at x=A/2?

📝 Show Solution

Find time to reach x = A/2

Formula: \( x(t) = A \cos\left(\frac{2\pi}{T}t\right) \)

Set \( x(t) = \frac{A}{2} \): \( \frac{A}{2} = A \cos\left(\frac{2\pi}{T}t\right) \)

\( \cos\left(\frac{2\pi}{T}t\right) = \frac{1}{2} \)

The smallest positive angle with cosine equal to 1/2 is \( \frac{\pi}{3} \)

\( \frac{2\pi}{T}t = \frac{\pi}{3} \implies t = \frac{T}{6} \)

Example 12 Math

Problem: y=12sin(2\(\pi\)t/10+\(\pi\)/4). Find A,f,y(1.25),v(2.5),a(5).

📝 Show Solution

Compare to standard form \( y = A \sin(2\pi ft + \phi) \)

From the equation: \( A = 12 \)

\( 2\pi f = \frac{2\pi}{10} \implies f = 0.1 \text{ Hz} \)

Find y(1.25):

\( y(1.25) = 12 \sin\left(\frac{2\pi(1.25)}{10} + \frac{\pi}{4}\right) = 12 \sin\left(\frac{\pi}{4} + \frac{\pi}{4}\right) = 12 \sin\left(\frac{\pi}{2}\right) = 12 \)

Find v(2.5):

Formula: \( v(t) = \frac{dy}{dt} = 12\left(\frac{2\pi}{10}\right) \cos\left(\frac{2\pi t}{10} + \frac{\pi}{4}\right) = 2.4\pi \cos\left(\frac{\pi t}{5} + \frac{\pi}{4}\right) \)

Substitution: \( v(2.5) = 2.4\pi \cos\left(\frac{2.5\pi}{5} + \frac{\pi}{4}\right) = 2.4\pi \cos\left(\frac{3\pi}{4}\right) = -5.33 \)

Find a(5):

Formula: \( a(t) = \frac{dv}{dt} = -12\left(\frac{2\pi}{10}\right)^2 \sin\left(\frac{2\pi t}{10} + \frac{\pi}{4}\right) = -0.48\pi^2 \sin\left(\frac{\pi t}{5} + \frac{\pi}{4}\right) \)

Substitution: \( a(5) = -0.48\pi^2 \sin\left(\frac{5\pi}{5} + \frac{\pi}{4}\right) = -0.48\pi^2 \sin\left(\frac{5\pi}{4}\right) = +3.35 \)

Ex 0.4 Math

Problem: Graph. m=0.25kg, A=0.2m, T=0.6s.

📝 Show Solution

Find angular frequency \( \omega \)

Formula: \( \omega = \frac{2\pi}{T} = \frac{2\pi}{0.6} = \frac{10\pi}{3} \text{ rad/s} \)

Equation of motion: \( x(t) = A\cos(\omega t) = 0.2\cos\left(\frac{10\pi}{3}t\right) \)

Find maximum velocity and acceleration

Formula: \( v_{max} = A\omega = 0.2\left(\frac{10\pi}{3}\right) = 2.094 \)

Formula: \( a_{max} = A\omega^2 = 0.2\left(\frac{10\pi}{3}\right)^2 = 21.93 \)

Find total energy E

Formula: \( E = \frac{1}{2} m \omega^2 A^2 \)

Substitution: \( E = \frac{1}{2}(0.25)\left(\frac{100\pi^2}{9}\right)(0.2^2) = 0.548 \text{ J} \)

Where does KE = PE?

When KE = PE, \( U = E/2 \implies \frac{1}{2} k x^2 = \frac{1}{2} \left(\frac{1}{2} k A^2\right) \)

\( x^2 = \frac{A^2}{2} \implies x = \pm\frac{A}{\sqrt{2}} = \pm\frac{0.2}{\sqrt{2}} = \pm 0.141 \text{ m} = \pm 14.1 \text{ cm} \)

Substitution: \( A = \sqrt{2(0.0715)/70} = 0.0452 \text{ m} = 4.52 \text{ cm} \)
