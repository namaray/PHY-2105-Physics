Example 1 Math

Problem: A block of mass 680g fastened to spring k=65 N/m, pulled 11cm. Find (a) \(\omega\), f, T; (b) A; (c) v_max.

📝 Show Solution

\( \omega=\sqrt{(65/0.68)}=9.78 \text{ rad/s} \)

\( f=1.56 \text{ Hz} \)

\( T=0.64s \)

\( A=0.11m \)

\( v_{max}=\omega A=1.1 \text{ m/s} \)

Example 2 Math

Problem: Spring stretches 3.90cm with 10.0g. 25.0g oscillates. Find (a) T; (b) f, \(\omega\).

📝 Show Solution

\( k=m_1g/\Delta y=2.513 \text{ N/m} \)

\( T=2\pi\sqrt{(0.025/2.513)}=0.627s \)

\( f=1.59Hz \)

\( \omega=10.0 \text{ rad/s} \)

Example 3 Math

Problem: 0.42kg, spring stretches 2.1cm, a=9.0 m/s². Find k.

📝 Show Solution

\( k=ma/x=0.42\times9.0/0.021=180 \text{ N/m} \)

Example 4 Math

Problem: m=0.30kg, k=19.6N/m, A=0.100m. Find (a) E; (b) K,U vs t; (c) v at x=0.050m; (d) K,U at x=A/2.

📝 Show Solution

\( E=\frac{1}{2}kA^2=9.80\times10^{-2} \text{ J} \)

\( v=\omega\sqrt{(A^2-x^2)}=0.70 \text{ m/s} \)

\( \text{at } A/2: U=2.45\times10^{-2} \text{ J}, K=7.35\times10^{-2} \text{ J} \)

Example 5 Math

Problem: 500g, A=20cm, T=0.80s. At what x is v=1.0 m/s?

📝 Show Solution

\( \omega=2\pi/0.80=7.85 \)

\( x=\pm\sqrt{(A^2-(v/\omega)^2)}=\pm15.4\text{cm} \)
